Part air gap
Height and spacing in mm (spacing = gap between raft fill lines — bigger is looser). Flow is a multiplier, speed in mm/s. Air gap is how far the part's first layer sits above the raft; raise it to 0.30–0.40 for a raft that lifts off in one sheet.
Supports
Supports are the projected "shadow" of everything overhanging by more than the angle, truncated at the part. Lower density = easier to snap off but weaker pillars; raise XY clearance if supports weld to the print. Any overhang shallower than the angle gets none.
